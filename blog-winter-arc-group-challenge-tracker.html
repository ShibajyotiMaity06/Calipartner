Winter Arc Group Challenge Tracker: Rules, Leaderboards & Squad Streaks

How to run a 90-day disciplined squad challenge with private leaderboards, macro floors, and zero excuses while everyone else hibernates.

By CaliPartner Editorial Team • Published November 2026 • 7 min read
Share with partner: WhatsApp Twitter / X

Every year from October 1st to January 1st, the majority of the world goes into hibernation. People skip morning workouts because it’s dark outside, indulge in holiday buffets, and promise themselves: “I'll start fresh in the New Year.” The Winter Arc is the radical rejection of that mindset: you lock in when it’s hardest, building an unbreakable physical and mental foundation before the rest of the world even makes a New Year's resolution.

Running the Winter Arc alone is notoriously brutal. Cold weather, shorter daylight hours, and holiday parties will test your willpower daily. But when you track your Winter Arc inside a private squad room, giving up ceases to be an option.

The 5 Non-Negotiable Winter Arc Standards

The Winter Arc is not about crash starvation or unsustainable 4-hour daily training. It is built on 5 deterministic daily standards executed relentlessly for 90 days:

1. Deterministic Daily Macro Floor

No more eyeballing portions or guessing calories. Calculate your baseline calories with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula and stick to your chosen direction: a controlled 300–400 kcal deficit for cutting or a clean 250 kcal surplus for lean hypertrophy.

The Daily Protein Floor: Consume at least 1.6g to 2.2g of protein per kilogram of body weight every single day. If you don't hit your protein floor, your day is incomplete.

2. The 8,000 to 10,000 Step Rule

Colder weather triggers an unconscious drop in Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT). People sit on the couch under blankets and drop from 10,000 daily steps to 2,500 without realizing it. Set an uncompromised step floor of 8,000 steps minimum. A 25-minute brisk walk after lunch or dinner gets you well past halfway.

3. 4 to 5 Heavy Resistance Training Sessions

Follow a progressive overload program (Push/Pull/Legs or Upper/Lower). Log your sets, reps, and working weight. In the Winter Arc, you train for strength and structural density.

4. 3 Litres of Daily Water

Because cold weather reduces the physical sensation of thirst by up to 40%, dehydration causes false hunger cravings and sluggish gym performance. Track your hydration in 500ml milestones throughout the day.

5. Daily Squad Room Check-In Before 10:30 PM

Silence is where habits die. In your Winter Arc squad room, every member logs their food, water, and workout before turning off the lights. Seeing your teammates' completed rings eliminates every excuse you made to procrastinate.

The Rule of Consistency: In CaliPartner, the Winter Arc leaderboard ranks members based on consecutive goal adherence, never on who ate the fewest calories or lost the most weight. Extreme restriction leads to rebound binging; sustainable discipline wins every time.

How to Set Up Your Winter Arc Squad Room in CaliPartner

You don't need complex spreadsheets, cluttered WhatsApp groups, or messy photo albums to run your challenge:

  • Create a Winter Arc Room: Host a squad room with up to 5 members (or 20 members on Pro).
  • Inspect Side-by-Side Days: Swipe between members to see their meals, water progress, and completed gym workouts.
  • Keep the Chat High-Signal: Post gym pump photos, meal scans, and motivational nudges directly inside your room chat.
  • 30-Day Protected Grace State: If a member travels or gets sick, the room's consistency streaks remain protected so nobody feels alienated.
