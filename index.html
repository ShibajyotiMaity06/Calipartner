New Macro Recalculation Engine

Goals change as you progress. Your targets should too.

As you lose body fat or gain muscle, your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) and metabolism shift. CaliPartner provides scheduled, proactive growth reviews so you never hit an unexplained plateau.

  • Plus Plan: Monthly Growth Review (30 Days). Analyzes your 30-day weight trend and consistency to recompute calories and macros for the upcoming month.
  • Pro Plan: Weekly AI Coach Review (7 Days). The AI Coach checks your 7-day rolling weight average, workout volume, and adherence to recommend refined macro distributions.
  • Strict Safety Floors Enforced. Calorie minimums (1,200 kcal women, 1,500 kcal men) and weekly rate caps (1% body weight/week) are never compromised.

Questions, answered

Is CaliPartner free to use?

Yes. Downloading and using CaliPartner for solo tracking (food diary, water, steps, workout logs, weight trends, and habit streaks) is completely free forever. You also get 1 free hosted room for you and an accountability partner (up to 2 people) at zero cost.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

The 7-day free trial is available exclusively on CaliPartner Plus (both monthly and annual). You are charged ₹0 / $0 upfront and have 7 full days to experience multi-person rooms, monthly growth checks, live focus sessions, and advanced micronutrients. You can cancel anytime before day 7 without being billed.

Why does Pro not have a free trial?

CaliPartner Pro utilizes cutting-edge vision models for AI photo food recognition and advanced Claude Haiku inference for personalized daily coaching. Because these incur immediate, non-trivial compute costs, Pro is billed immediately upon signup with 0 trial days.

Do both of us need to pay for a room?

No! CaliPartner uses a Host Pays model. The room creator's tier covers the whole room. If you subscribe to Plus or Pro, your invited partners or friends join and participate completely free.

What happens if a room host cancels or their plan lapses?

The room enters a protected 30-day grace period. Streaks, routines, workouts, and chat history are never lost. Any room member can sponsor the room by upgrading their own plan to reactivate it immediately.

What are Dynamic Growth Checks?

Nutrition isn't one-size-fits-all, and calorie needs change as you lose weight or build muscle. Plus users receive a guided review every 30 days to adjust targets, while Pro users receive active weekly adjustments from their AI Coach. Strict safety floors (1,200 kcal women, 1,500 kcal men) are always preserved.

Can I use CaliPartner on the web?

Yes! Our full web application is live at app.calipartner.site. You can sign in and start tracking immediately from any browser on phone, tablet, or laptop.
