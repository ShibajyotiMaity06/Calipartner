See the day as two halves, joined by one seam

The Room screen puts you on the left and your partner on the right. A single line runs between you. It stays dashed until both of you have logged, then it lights up.

  • Percent, not calories. Comparisons show percent of each person's own goal, so nobody is measured against someone else's numbers.
  • Identity colors, not score colors. Lime and ice cyan belong to each person. There is no red for losing and no green for winning.
  • Nudges that feel friendly. One tap sends a pre-written, kind reminder, limited so it never turns into nagging.

You decide what your partner sees

Sharing is set per room and per data type, with conservative defaults. Changes apply immediately and hide earlier items too.

  • Eye icon on every shared item that explains in plain words what the other person can see.
  • Hidden means hidden. Locked items show a neutral lock, never a zero.
  • Delete anytime. Remove your account and data from inside the app.

Ranked on consistency, never on eating least

Weekly leaderboards are split into divisions and small cohorts so a beginner never competes with the global top 1%.

  • Divisions: cutting, bulking, maintain and beginner.
  • Seasonal challenges like Winter Arc, and occasion plans for birthdays, trips and weddings.
  • Opt out any time. Opted-out users are hidden entirely.

Questions, answered

Is CaliPartner free?

Downloading is free and basic solo logging of food, water, steps, workouts and weight stays free forever. New users get a 3-day trial of rooms, then rooms need a paid plan.

Do both of us need to pay?

Pricing is per user, not per room. Each person who wants to use a room needs an active trial or plan. If one partner's plan ends, the room pauses for both and the streak is saved for 30 days.

Can we have different goals?

Yes. One of you can be cutting while the other bulks. Comparisons are always shown as a percent of each person's own goal.

What can my partner see about me?

Only what you choose. By default your partner sees whether you logged today and your goal completion percent. Calories, individual meals and your weight number stay hidden unless you opt in.

How are calorie targets calculated?

With fixed, published formulas shown on screen so you can see your inputs and result. They are estimates for healthy adults, not medical advice.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions bought in the app are managed in Google Play or the App Store. See our refund and cancellation policy for the steps.

How do I delete my account?

Open Me, then Account, then Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
