Fitness is easier with someone beside you

Most trackers are built for one person and most people quit within weeks. We built CaliPartner around the one thing that keeps people going: a person.

Who it is for

Adults 18 and over who train and eat with intention: gym partners, couples, friend groups running a challenge, and solo users who want to add someone later. We are starting in India and expanding to English-speaking markets.

What we believe

Companionship beats competition. Nobody should feel they are losing. Fitness data is personal, so it stays private until you share it.

Our product principles

Compare to your own goal

Never to other people's numbers.

Private by default

You opt in to sharing calories, weight and meals.

Never reward undereating

No ranking, badge or streak is won by eating less.

Logging takes seconds

Repeat foods are one tap.

A room survives absence

One quiet member never ends the room.

Explainable numbers

Targets come from published formulas you can see.

CaliPartner is a fitness and habit-tracking tool and not a medical service. It is not intended for medical or clinical nutrition, pregnancy or eating-disorder treatment. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise.
