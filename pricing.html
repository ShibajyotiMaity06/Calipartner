Dynamic Growth Checks & Macro Recalculation

Nutrition goals are not static. As you drop fat or build muscle, your TDEE shifts. CaliPartner ensures you never hit a wall.

Plan TierRecalculation CadenceHow It Works
Free (Basic) On-Demand (Manual) Re-trigger the onboarding calculator from settings at any time based on updated scale weight.
Plus Plan Every Month (30 Days) App prompts a Monthly Growth Review: analyzes weight trends, verifies goal pace, and calculates updated macro targets for confirmation.
Pro Plan Every Week (7 Days) The AI Coach actively reviews rolling 7-day weights, workout volume, and compliance, recommending micro calorie and macro refinements to prevent plateaus.
Safety Enforcement: In both Plus and Pro, calorie floors (1,200 kcal women, 1,500 kcal men) and weekly rate caps (maximum 1% body weight per week) remain strictly enforced.

Key Billing & Architectural Rules

Room Sponsorship Model

The room host's plan determines room capacity. Invited members do not need to pay to participate. If a host's subscription lapses, the room enters a 30-day protected grace state where any member can upgrade to sponsor it.

7-Day Free Trial on Plus

Available exclusively on Plus (both monthly and annual). Cancel anytime before day 7 in your store or web dashboard and you will not be billed.

Pro Immediate Billing

Pro has 0 trial days due to underlying Claude Haiku and computer vision compute costs. Billing begins immediately upon subscription confirmation.

Privacy and Safety

Strangers in Partner Finder only see banded demographics (age bracket, fitness goal, training style, city)—never your weight, calories, or exact location before mutual pairing.
