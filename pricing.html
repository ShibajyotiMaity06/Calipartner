How billing works

3-day free trial

Every new user gets 3 days to try rooms, chat and streaks, including one room of 2 people.

Billed by the store

In-app purchases go through Google Play Billing and Apple In-App Purchase. Restore purchases is available on both.

Cancel anytime

Manage or cancel in your store subscription settings. See the refund and cancellation policy.

If a partner's plan ends

The room pauses for both people and your streak is saved for 30 days. Your own diary and logging are never affected. The partner who lapsed sees a simple Rejoin your room screen.
