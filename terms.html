1. About the service

CaliPartner (“we”, “us”) is operated by CaliPartner, India. CaliPartner is a mobile app and web application for tracking food, water, steps, workouts, and body progress, and for sharing selected information with other people in private “rooms”. The service is delivered digitally; there is no physical product or shipping.

4. Plans, billing and free trial

  • Free Tier (Basic): Personal logging of food, water, steps, workouts and weight, your diary, personal progress, personal streaks, and your free 1-on-1 partner room (capacity 2 people) are free forever.
  • Plus Plan: The habit and community powerhouse. India: ₹249 per month or ₹1,499 per year (~50% annual savings). Global: $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year (~53% annual savings). Includes a 7-day free trial on both monthly and annual plans. Features hosting up to 3 rooms (up to 5 people per room), monthly macro growth checks (every 30 days), full shared diary, send & read room text chat, shared workouts, live focus sessions, room leaderboards, fasting timer, and advanced micronutrient tracking.
  • Pro Plan: The elite AI-coached nutrition and body transformation tier. India: ₹499 per month or ₹2,499 per year (~58% annual savings). Global: $9.99 per month or $49.00 per year (~61% annual savings). No free trial (0 days) due to high underlying vision and language model compute costs. Billed immediately upon confirmation. Features hosting up to 5 rooms (up to 20 people per room), weekly AI growth & macro recalculation checks (every 7 days), context-aware Personal AI Coach (10 msgs/day), AI Photo Food Logging (5 scans/day), Chat-to-Log, “Fits My Day” AI recipe collections, room photo & video sharing, weekly AI progress summaries, and priority support.
  • Room Sponsorship Model (Host Pays): The room creator/host's plan determines the room capacity (Free: 2, Plus: 5, Pro: 20 members). Invited members join any paid room for free without needing their own subscription. If a host's plan lapses, the room enters a 30-day grace/paused state where any member can sponsor it, preserving all streaks and history.

  • Payment processing: In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, Apple In-App Purchase, or RevenueCat. Web purchases are processed by our payment partner, Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record for web transactions.
  • Refunds: See our refund and cancellation policy.
