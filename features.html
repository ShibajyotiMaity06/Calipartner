Built for two. Engineered for consistency.

Food, water, steps, workouts, progress and AI coaching, with a shared accountability room at the center.

1. Core Tracking & Daily Diary

100% offline-first logging with Indian foods first and transparent formulas.

  • 5 Meal Slots: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, and Extra.
  • 70,000+ Nutrition Database: Indian foods (IFCT) prioritized alongside global staples.
  • Barcode Scanner: Fast camera and image gallery barcode recognition.
  • Custom Foods & My Meals: Save personal recipes and repeat meals in one tap.
  • Safety Floors: Hard floors (1,200 kcal women, 1,500 kcal men) strictly enforced.
  • Water Logging: Quick-add with smart volume reminders based on weight.
  • Steps & Distance: Seamless integration with Apple Health and Health Connect.
  • GPS Activity Tracking: Live route, distance, and pace for walks, runs, and rides.
  • Workout Logging: Catalog of 300+ exercises, sets, reps, and volume history.
  • Weight Tracking & Trends: 7-day moving averages that smooth out water retention.
  • Personal Focus Timer: Stopwatch & pomodoro timer for focused study/work blocks.
  • Offline-First SQLite: Logging never hangs or requires an active connection.

2. Social, Accountability & Rooms

A private room where you and your people stay in it together without toxic competition.

  • Room Capacity: Free: 2 people | Plus: 5 people | Pro: 20 people.
  • Room Sponsorship Model: Host pays, invited members join free.
  • Free 1-on-1 Partner Room: Included free forever on all accounts.
  • Two Halves, One Seam: You on the left, your partner on the right.
  • Percent, Not Calories: Comparisons based on % of individual goal achieved.
  • Identity Hues: Iris (Person A) and Teal (Person B)—never green for winning or red for losing.
  • Room Text Chat: Read-only on Free; Send & Read on Plus and Pro.
  • Room Media Sharing: Share photos and workout videos (Pro exclusive).
  • Live Shared Focus Sessions: Study or work simultaneously with peers.
  • Room-Only Leaderboard: Filtered cohort ranks for just your room members.
  • Pair & Seasonal Challenges: 1, 7, and 30-day challenges (Winter Arc, etc.).
  • Partner Finder: Deterministic matching engine (gender, goal, age band).

3. Nutrition, Wellness & Content

Curated recipes, intermittent fasting windows, and comprehensive body metrics.

  • Recipe Library: 10 starter recipes on Free; 150+ macro-balanced recipes on Plus/Pro.
  • “Fits My Day” AI Collections: Smart recipes that hit your remaining daily macros (Pro).
  • Fasting Tracker: 16:8, 18:6, and custom intermittent fasting timers with notifications.
  • Advanced Micronutrients: Fibre, saturated fat, sodium, cholesterol, and sugar tracking.
  • Meal Planner & Auto Grocery: Plan week-long splits and auto-compile shopping lists.
  • Workout Routines: Save multi-day split programs with exercise progressions.
  • Auto Rest Timer & PRs: Countdown rest intervals and automated 1RM / PR tracking.
  • Body Circumferences: Chest, waist, and hip tape measurement trend lines.
  • Private Photo Vault: Encrypted side-by-side visual progress comparison.
  • Full Data Export: Export your full nutrition, workout, and biometric data to JSON/CSV.

4. AI Intelligence & Coaching

Context-aware nutrition intelligence and automated metabolic recalculation.

  • Personal AI Coach: Context-aware Claude Haiku coach (10 msgs/day on Pro).
  • AI Photo Food Logging: Snap a photo to estimate multi-item portions and macros (5 scans/day on Pro).
  • Chat-to-Log: Type “I ate 2 boiled eggs and toast with chai” to auto-log instantly.
  • Monthly Macro Growth Check: 30-day guided recalculation on Plus based on weight trend.
  • Weekly AI Growth Review: 7-day rolling adherence and metabolic review on Pro.
  • Weekly AI Progress Summary: Deep-dive metabolic and consistency analysis every Sunday.
  • Priority Support Queue: Direct fast-track customer support for Pro members.
