Community guidelines

Rooms are small, private spaces for people who support each other. These rules keep them that way.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Be supportive

  • Encourage progress. Do not shame anyone for what they eat, weigh or miss.
  • Respect each person's own goal. A cut and a bulk are equally valid.
  • Do not pressure anyone to share data they have chosen to keep private.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Sexual, graphic, violent or exploitative images or messages.
  • Content that promotes eating disorders, extreme restriction, purging or unsafe weight loss.
  • Impersonation, spam, scams, advertising or selling products.
  • Sharing someone else's private information or images without their consent.
  • Any content involving minors in an unsafe or inappropriate context.

Your safety tools

  • Report a message, profile or username from within the app.
  • Block a person so they cannot find, invite or request you.
  • Mute a member to hide their nudges, reactions and chat from you.
  • Leave any room at any time.
  • Choose “Nobody” under who can find you by username.

How we enforce

Reports go to a moderation queue. Depending on severity we may remove content, warn, restrict chat or suspend the account. Serious violations may be reported to the authorities. Send urgent reports to support@calipartner.app.

If you or someone you know is struggling with an eating disorder or in crisis, please contact a local helpline or a medical professional right away.
